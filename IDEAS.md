# 🧬 Pulse: Future Horizons

The ChatGPT Exporter is becoming the **Pulse Engine**. Here are the next-level arcs to transform "exporting" into "knowledge ownership."

### 🚀 Arc 1: The Invisible Harvester (Sync)
- **Pulse Auto-Sync**: A background service that detects new chats and auto-downloads them to a local `Vault/` folder (or Obsidian) every time you close a tab.
- **Zero-Click Backups**: Connect to Google Drive or OneDrive. Your chats are archived before you even realize it.

### 🧠 Arc 2: The Local Brain (Obsidian + RAG)
- **Deep Obsidian Plugin**: A dedicated Obsidian plugin that reads the Pulse format and provides **AI-powered search** over your own thoughts.
- **Synthesized History**: Ask: *"What was my project idea from Gemini last month when I was talking about React?"* and Pulse finds the exact text across all 3 platforms.

### 📊 Arc 3: Prompt Engineering Analytics
- **Prompt Heatmaps**: Visualize your efficiency. See where you get "hallucinations" vs. "high-quality" outputs based on prompt length and complexity.
- **Template Library**: Extract your best prompts into a reusable library. Pulse becomes your "Prompt Manager."

### 🎨 Arc 4: Collaborative Portals
- **Shared Projects**: Export an entire project and share a **Live HTML Portal** with a teammate (styled just like ChatGPT).
- **Public Portfolio**: Generate a "Best Of" page to show off your AI interactions on GitHub.
